# Third-party attribution and redistribution notes

This student repository documents work based on the Brain2Qwerty V1 research
workflow and the community Brain2Qwerty V1 Beta bundle.

## Sources

- Original paper: *Noninvasive decoding of typed sentences from human brain
  activity*, Nature Neuroscience (2026), DOI:
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02303-2
- Original research code lineage:
  https://github.com/facebookresearch/brain2qwerty
- Community reproduction bundle:
  https://huggingface.co/farmakohealth/brain2qwerty-v1-beta
- Dataset referenced by the community bundle:
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/bcbl190626/SpanishBCBL

## License statement from the community bundle

The community bundle's README identifies that release as **CC BY-NC 4.0** for
non-commercial research use. It attributes its modifications to Farmako Health
and asks users to retain attribution to the original Brain2Qwerty authors and
SpanishBCBL data creators and to follow the dataset's terms.

The inclusion of selected community scripts and metadata in this student
repository is for non-commercial educational/research documentation and does
not imply ownership or endorsement. Users should consult the upstream bundle,
original code repository, and dataset terms before reuse or redistribution.

## Excluded materials

The large language-model binary, neural checkpoints, prediction CSVs, raw MEG
recordings, credentials, and local environment/build folders are not included.
Prediction CSVs are excluded pending a separate review of their redistribution
terms.
